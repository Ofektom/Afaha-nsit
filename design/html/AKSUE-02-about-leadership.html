Our story

From college to a specialised university of education

Akwa Ibom State University of Education builds on a long tradition of teacher training in the state. Its establishment as a university marks a new chapter: degree-awarding status, expanded research capacity and a mandate to raise the quality of teaching across every level of education.

Today the University offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across six faculties and schools, combining strong subject knowledge with pedagogy, supervised teaching practice and digital literacy.
