University unveils roadmap for a fully digital campus by 2027

The phased programme begins with a new official website, followed by online admissions, course registration, results and Remita-powered fee payments.

Directorate of Information & Protocol
Official university communication
Feature photo · VC with management at the briefing
The Vice-Chancellor briefing principal officers on the digital transformation programme. Photo: Information Unit
IN THIS ARTICLE
Overview
Phase 1: Website
Phase 2–4: Portals
What students should do

The University Management has approved a comprehensive digital transformation programme that will move core academic and administrative services online. The programme was unveiled at a briefing chaired by the Vice-Chancellor on Monday.

Phase 1: A new official website

The first phase delivers a modern, mobile-friendly website with complete faculty and department information, profiles of principal officers and a central newsroom for official announcements.

“No student should have to travel to campus or queue at a bank to complete a process that can be done securely online.”
— Prof. [Full Name], Vice-Chancellor

Phases 2–4: Integrated portals

Subsequent phases introduce the Admissions Portal, Course Registration & Results, and a Fee Payment portal integrated with Remita. All portals will share a single student identity, so one login gives access to every service.

What students should do

Students and applicants are advised to rely only on official University channels for announcements, and to pay fees exclusively through Remita RRR once payment goes online.
