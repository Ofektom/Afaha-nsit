About the department

Counsellors for every school

The Department of Guidance & Counselling prepares graduates to provide educational, vocational and personal-social guidance in primary and secondary schools, tertiary institutions and community agencies. Students complete supervised counselling practicum alongside teaching practice.

Mission
Produce ethical, skilled counsellors.
Accreditation
Full NUC accreditation (sample).
Practicum
Placement in 40+ partner schools.
